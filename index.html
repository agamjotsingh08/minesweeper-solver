What are we measuring?

Each board starts with a guaranteed empty opening. The solver applies clue constraints, the total mine count, and subset subtraction until the board is solved or no further deduction is available. A stalled board is unresolved by this engine; it is not necessarily impossible for a stronger solver. Verified mode filters out these stalled boards before play.

Learn to prove, not guess.

Practice telling certainty from uncertainty. These small positions have no hidden answer board: the checker enumerates every mine arrangement consistent with the clues.

These are possible arrangements, not guesses about a secret board. Two opposing examples prove ambiguity. One example alone cannot prove certainty; the checker exhausts every arrangement in these small lessons.

A QUICK FIELD GUIDE

Follow the numbers.

Reveal all safe cells to win. A number tells you how many mines touch that cell, including diagonally.

  • Click a cell to reveal it. Right-click to flag it. On touch screens, enable Flag mode.
  • Arrow keys move between cells. Enter / Space reveals. F toggles a flag.
  • Step applies one round of proven deductions. Hint highlights the cells involved without playing them.
  • Run AI solver plays consecutive rounds. Pause at any time and inspect the journal.
  • The replay slider lets you review past moves. Select Live to resume playing.

Learn with MineMind

Open Learn for five short reasoning exercises, including a position where two opposing mine arrangements prove that a conclusion is uncertain. Enable Proof coach to block manual reveals unless the solver proves them safe. Export reasoning downloads only public clues, known mines, and deductions, never the hidden board or seed.

Why “verified no-guess”?

Ordinary Minesweeper can require a guess. Verified mode generates and tests boards until this solver can clear one entirely by logic from the supplied safe opening. Classic mode accepts the first random board and stops if the available rules cannot prove another move.

Your flags are annotations, not facts: the AI only trusts mines it proves itself. Progress is saved in this browser when storage is available.
